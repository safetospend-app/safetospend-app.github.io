How it works

4 simple steps
  1. 1
    Define your habitsTap “+ Add daily habit” (up to 20) and set a month goal — how many days you want to do it this month.
  2. 2
    Assign a categoryPick Health, Fitness, Study… to see which area of life is going well. Edit categories and emojis in Settings.
  3. 3
    Tick off as you goTap a box each day you complete a habit. Charts, weekly rings and progress bars update instantly.
  4. 4
    Repeat monthlyWhen a new month starts, tap “Copy habits” to bring everything over with a clean slate. Tap the calendar icon to see your whole year.
Weekly habits

Each week has its own list (up to 20). Add things you want to do once that week, tick them when done, and use “Copy week” to repeat last week’s list.

Install it like an app
  1. 📱
    iPhone / iPadOpen this page in Safari → tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”.
  2. 🤖
    AndroidOpen in Chrome → tap ⋮ → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. 💻
    ComputerIn Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar. Works offline once installed.
Your data

Everything is saved privately on this device — no account needed. To move to another device, open Settings → Export backup, then Import it there. Want it on paper? Settings → Print this page.
