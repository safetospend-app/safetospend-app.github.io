Bills & the calendar

Set a bill once. It shows up every month.

Rent, subscriptions, insurance, loan payments: add them once with their due date. They fill your calendar, post themselves on the day, or wait for you to tick them off. Anything overdue moves to the top of your overview.

  • Weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month, monthly, quarterly, yearly
  • Savings and debt payments move money into the right account
  • Account balances update with every transaction and transfer

Questions

Good to know.

How do I get the app after buying?

Your download is a short PDF with your personal link. Open the link on your phone and add it to your home screen; the PDF shows the steps for iPhone and Android. It takes under a minute.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device. There's no account, no bank connection and nothing is sent to a server. You can download a backup file anytime and restore it on a new phone.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first visit, The Annual opens and works offline.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, Mac and Windows computers, in any modern browser. Each device keeps its own data; use backup and restore to move it.

Can my partner and I use it together?

Yes. Add up to 6 people, tag who spent what, and filter every page by person. To share one budget, use it on one device or move a backup between devices.

Does it work with my currency?

Yes, every currency is supported, and your budget year can start in any month.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The free demo is the full app with sample data, so you can tap around before you buy.
