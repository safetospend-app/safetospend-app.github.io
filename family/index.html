Questions

Which tablets does it work on?

Any iPad, Android or Fire tablet with an up-to-date browser — plus phones and computers. Landscape on a 10″ or bigger tablet looks best on the wall.

Does it sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. Paste your calendar’s private iCal link (Google, Apple iCloud or Outlook) and its events show up on the wall, updated automatically every 15 minutes. To change those events, edit them in the original calendar.

Can everyone update it from their phones?

Calendar events, yes — add them to your Google, Apple or Outlook calendar and they appear on the wall. Chores, rewards, meals and lists are kept on the wall tablet itself.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser — there’s no account to make. You can download a backup file anytime and restore it on another tablet.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours forever.

Can I try it first?

Yes — the demo is the full app with a sample family. Play with it as long as you like.
