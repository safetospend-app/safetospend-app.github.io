Privacy policy

Bloom Budget · by ThePetalo · last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

Bloom Budget is a budget app that runs in your browser. There is no account, no server and no tracking. Your budget is saved only on your own device. If you choose to connect Google Sheets, the app writes a copy of your budget into a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive — and nowhere else.

Data stored on your device

  • Everything you enter (plan, money log, bills, goals, settings) is saved in your browser’s storage on your device.
  • We (ThePetalo) never receive, see or store this data. There are no analytics, ads or third-party trackers in the app.
  • You can download a backup file, restore it, or delete everything at any time in Settings.

Google Sheets connection (optional)

  • Only if you tap Connect Google Sheets, Google asks you to let Bloom Budget access “only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app” (scope drive.file). Bloom Budget cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.
  • The app creates one spreadsheet called “Bloom Budget” in your Drive and writes your budget data into it (overview, plan, money log, goals). It also reads your Google account e-mail address to show which account is connected.
  • The connection runs directly between your device and Google. The Google access token is kept on your device only and expires after about one hour.
  • Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide this sync feature to you. It is not shared, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model. Bloom Budget’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • To disconnect, tap Disconnect in Settings. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your spreadsheet stays in your Drive until you delete it.
