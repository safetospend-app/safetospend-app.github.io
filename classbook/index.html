Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and there's no account. You can download a backup file anytime and restore it on another device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it's opened, it keeps working with no internet.

Is it a PDF or GoodNotes planner?

No. It's an interactive app that opens in your browser and can be installed like an app. It can print any page or save it as PDF.

Can I use it for homeschool?

Yes. Choose Homeschool mode when you start, and the app hides the pages you won't need.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours to keep, with free updates.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The demo is the full app with sample data. Changes reset when you reload it.
