Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere, and there’s no account to make. You can download a backup file anytime and restore it on another device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it’s open, it keeps working with no internet — on a plane, on the metro, anywhere.

Phone or computer?

Both. It’s built for phone screens and big screens. Each device keeps its own copy, and you can move data between them with the backup file.

Do I need to install anything?

No app store, no install required. Open it in any modern browser; if you want it on your home screen, your browser can add it with one tap.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours forever.

Can I try it first?

Yes — the demo is the full app with sample data. Play with it as long as you like.
